Share a case

Talk it through like you're telling a friend, or type it out. Good ones become new patients for everyone to practise on.

Things to mention

  1. Who they are. Age, job, sport or hobbies.
  2. What happened. How it started and how long ago.
  3. What they feel. Where it hurts, what it feels like, anything like pins and needles.
  4. What makes it worse or better. Sitting, stairs, sleep, rest.
  5. What the tests showed. Which checks gave a clue and which were normal.
  6. The answer. What it turned out to be, and anything you had to rule out first.

Please don't use real names or anything that could identify a real patient.

Send it opens a short form on GitHub with your case already filled in. If you don't have a GitHub account, copy the text instead and send it however suits you.
